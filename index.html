🛡️ Stay Safe Online

  • Never share passwords, banking PINs or verification codes.
  • Avoid schemes that promise guaranteed income or unusually high returns.
  • Research a platform before paying any fees.
  • Never pay a stranger to unlock supposed earnings or withdrawals.
  • Remember: online work takes time, effort and practice. Income is not guaranteed.
